Part 3

Sign in to VERVE

The new VERVE runs on a test site with sample data. There's no password: you sign in with a link sent to your email. It takes a few minutes.

  1. Open the test site

    Go to colab.riworks.app.

  2. Enter your coLAB email

    Use your @colabimpact.com address. The site only knows that one.

  3. Click the link in your email

    Open the email from VERVE and click the sign-in link. Use it within a few minutes, in the same browser.

    No email after five minutes? Check your spam folder, then send Ken a screenshot of the sign-in page.

  4. Send Ken a screenshot

    Once you're in, press ⌘ + Shift + 4, then Space, and click the browser window.

What will I see?

Your account is set up to see every level at once, so you can try each one: the System level, coLAB, PANO, and two sample clients, PANO Client 1 and VERVE Client 1.

Everything here is sample data. Change whatever you like; Ken can reset it.

Questions: ask Ken.
