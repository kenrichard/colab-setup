Part 4

Get a token

A token is a password the screens on your Mac use to sign in to the test site as you. Part 5 needs it. This takes two minutes. Do Part 3 first, and do Part 5 straight after.

  1. Open Settings on the test site

    Go to colab.riworks.app/settings, signed in as in Part 3. You can also get there from Settings in the menu under your name.

  2. Issue a token

    Under What is it for, type My Mac. Leave Read-only unticked. Click Issue a token.

  3. Copy it and keep the tab open

    Click the copy button beside the token. It's shown only once, so leave this tab open and go straight on to Part 5.

    No screenshot for this part. The token works like a password, so don't send it to anyone, Ken included. Part 5's screenshot shows it worked.

What if I lose it or close the tab?

Nobody can show it to you again, including Ken, because the site keeps only a scrambled copy. Go back to Settings, click Revoke beside My Mac, and issue a new one.
